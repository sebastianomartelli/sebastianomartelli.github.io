Welcome. I am a PhD student in Economics and Finance in the joint programme between the University of Trento and the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano. My research lies at the intersection of labour economics, education economics, and the economics of inequality.

I study how schooling, family background, and institutional design shape the distribution of economic opportunity.

I am from Ravenna, and before the PhD I studied in Bologna, Rome, and Stockholm. I also worked as a research assistant at Sapienza University of Rome. You can read more about my work on the research page, or download my CV.
