Welcome. I am a PhD candidate in Economics and Finance at the University of Trento and the Free University of Bolzano. I am a labour economist, and most of my work is about education and the economics of inequality.

My research asks how schooling, family background, and institutional design shape the distribution of economic opportunity.

Before the PhD I read economics at the University of Bologna and worked as a research assistant linking administrative tax and school records. You can read more about my work on the research page, or download my CV.
